## 🚀 My Journey

I started programming at 19 and haven't stopped since. I enjoy discovering new technologies and languages, and I try to stay up to date.

**🌱 The beginning:** PHP, HTML, CSS and MySQL, building websites and experimenting with code. Then I found **CodeIgniter**, my first framework, which gave me the opportunity to build a university website and blog. There I learned **SQL Server, .NET and C#**, and worked with **Azure** to deploy systems and help improve the infrastructure of the Digital Education department at [UCP](https://www.ucp.edu.ar).

**🐦 VencejosLab:** After 5 years, a partner and I started working as freelancers and founded [VencejosLab](https://www.vencejoslab.com), building software for clients with all kinds of needs. I'm no longer running it, but I still help out on the occasional project.

**🇨🇷 [WolkSoft](https://wolksoftcr.com) (Costa Rica):** For 3 years I built mobile apps with **C#, Vue.js, Ionic and Angular**. Something I really enjoyed there was setting up **CI/CD on Azure** to automate a big part of our work.

**🏢 Provisions Group (today):** Full-stack work with **Angular, TypeScript, Python/Django and AWS Lambda**, and, little by little, more of a tech-lead role. I also keep collaborating on projects with **.NET Core, Vue.js and PHP**.

**🎓 Back to UCP:** I owe a lot to UCP. First, it was where I worked and grew professionally, and that job allowed me to start my family. Later, I went back to my studies there and finally graduated as an Information Systems Engineer. Now I've returned as a teaching assistant to do my small part, helping 5th-year students in their education. It's my way of saying thank you.
